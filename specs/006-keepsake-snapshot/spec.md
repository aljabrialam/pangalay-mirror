# Feature Specification: Keepsake Snapshot

**Feature Branch**: `006-keepsake-snapshot`
**Status**: Approved under owner's instruction to proceed with remaining makeathon features
**Input**: F9 from `CODEX-FEATURES.md`.

## User Story
After finishing a round, the visitor can download a 1080×1350 PNG keepsake featuring the selected avatar in the hands-overhead pose, dusk colors, Magsukul with its approved Sulat Sūg spelling, Pangalay Mirror, and score.

## Requirements
- Generate the image locally in the browser; do not upload or persist a webcam image.
- Use the selected avatar and overhead pose.
- Include `مَغْسُكُلْ`, the title, and score.
- Keep the original concept film unchanged.
