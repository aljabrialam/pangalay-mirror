Pangalay Mirror

فَڠَلَيْ
Step in. Move. Meet the Tausug.
A webcam dance game that teaches pangalay, the dance of my people, the Tausug of Sulu, and lets you catch four treasures of our culture as you move. An avatar mirrors your body, a coach shows each gesture, and every catch pauses the dance to tell that treasure's story, spoken aloud and written in our own script.
My entry for COMMON GROUND, where culture meets technology. I want to preserve the Tausug language, art and culture through technology, and introduce my people to the world.
