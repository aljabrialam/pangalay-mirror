How it works
The eyes

Webcam tracking

Follows your body and hands in the browser. A laptop is enough.

The game

Follow the coach

Pictograms show the next move. Gold moves catch a Tausug treasure. The dance pauses while its story is told.

The memory

Recorded voices

Each object says its name in Tausug and shows its cousin in the Malay world.

You learn the dance by playing, and meet the culture as you score.

This is a concept animation of the installation, not the working build. The gestures and patterns follow published descriptions of pangalay and Tausug art. The gesture names are plain English descriptions, not Tausug terms. The attire and object names should be checked by the team before presenting. Sound is off until you turn it on. The music is the Pangalay track supplied by the team, and the spoken descriptions use a Philippine English voice (Rosa) standing in for a recorded Tausug speaker.
